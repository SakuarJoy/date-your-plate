# date-your-plate
In 14 October I joined the workshop "From Real-World Problems to AI-Powered Solutions" organized by Google and Junior Achievement, where I and my team created this mok-up based on the problem "I don't know what I have or what to buy". This should be an app but we had only 45min te create something so I made this website.
## Idea behind it
We started with the idea that organizing a household is difficult and keeping track of everything even more so. With this application you can scan (with AI) your recite and AI will add it on the list, it will also remind you if something needs to be refilled or will turn bad.
